## Boundary notes

- Keep raw recordings, signed consent, private correspondence, calendars, identifiable images and institution-restricted files outside this profile unless they are explicitly controlled and necessary.
- If a source is inspected, record what was looked at and what it supports in `evidence_log.md`.
- A source pointer does not make a claim true; preserve limitations and uncertainty.
