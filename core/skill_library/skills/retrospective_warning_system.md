# Skill: Retrospective Warning System

## Purpose

Turn lessons from earlier academic and design work into checks that prevent recurring weaknesses in a new project.

## Warning patterns

### 1. The concept appears suddenly

Show the path from evidence and insight to criteria, alternatives, selection and iteration. A final idea without a visible decision trail is difficult to assess or reuse.

### 2. Testing is described but not evidenced

Record the test question, setup, participants or materials, interaction, observation, interpretation, change and limitation. “Feedback was positive” is not a testing record.

### 3. Impact is implied

Name who or what may benefit, through which mechanism, under what conditions and with what evidence. Separate desired impact from observed impact.

### 4. System framing arrives too late

Identify relevant actors, relationships, flows, power, resources and feedback before selecting an intervention. Do not retrofit a system story around a finished artefact.

### 5. Complexity is compressed into a slogan

Use concise language only when the underlying map, assumptions and limitations remain available.

### 6. Criteria are hidden

State the criteria used to compare ideas. Record rejected alternatives and why they were rejected.

### 7. Evidence types are mixed

Distinguish desk sources, participant accounts, observation, researcher interpretation, synthetic examples, mock data and validated claims.

## Milestone checklist

- [ ] Is the project question open enough to investigate?
- [ ] Is each major claim traceable to appropriate evidence?
- [ ] Are evidence, interpretation, hypothesis and desired future separated?
- [ ] Is the system or context visible at the level relevant to the project?
- [ ] Are criteria and alternatives explicit?
- [ ] Does every prototype have a learning question and testing record?
- [ ] Is the intended impact distinct from demonstrated impact?
- [ ] Are limitations visible in the main output?
- [ ] Are personal and participant materials stored outside the reusable core?
