# Semester 2 Taught-Method Catalog

Scope: methods taught through systems, futures, transition and design-experiment sessions.

## Status and use

This is a reusable record of taught methodological capability, not a secondary-research evidence bank. Course PDFs are not bundled in this repository. The course filenames and physical page anchors below are source locators; verify the original page before relying on an exact quotation, diagram or definition.

## Core methods

| Method | Course anchor | What it structures | Main output | Important limit | Application note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Systems Mapping | `Week 4`, pp. 4–15; core components p. 6 | Parts, connections, dynamics, information flows, feedback loops and system purpose | System map plus leverage/feedback hypotheses | A drawn connection is not automatically an observed causal fact | Use it to expose structure before selecting an intervention |
| Three Horizons (3H) | `Week 8`, p. 20 and pp. 31–39 | Current pattern (H1), desired future (H3) and transitional innovation (H2); separates H2− from H2+ | Transition map and `From → To` statement | H3 is a desired trajectory, not a forecast; H2 needs testing | Distinguish reproduction, transition and aspiration |
| Theory of Change | `Week 10`, pp. 24–26 | Situation, aims, inputs, activities, outputs, mechanisms, outcomes, impact, evidence, assumptions and unintended consequences | Testable change logic | It does not prove the proposed mechanism | Make assumptions and evidence gaps visible |
| Change Map / backcasting | `Week 10`, pp. 27–28 | Pathway from a desired future back to present preconditions and interventions | Staged roadmap | Sequencing depends on ownership, resources and constraints | Mark unknown preconditions instead of inventing them |
| Design Experiments | `Week 10`, pp. 29–33 | Insight → intervention → expected behaviour/experience → evaluation method | Hypothesis, variables, criteria and learning plan | A small exploratory test is not causal proof | Bound the learning question and test conditions |
| Power–Interest Stakeholder Mapping | `Week 10`, pp. 16–17 | Stakeholder power, interest, values and engagement approach | Provisional engagement map | Power and interest must be evidenced | Mark uncertain positions as hypotheses |
| Futures Wheel | `Week 8`, p. 17 | Primary, secondary and tertiary consequences of a change | Opportunity/risk chain and safeguards | Consequences are hypotheses unless observed or tested | Use it to identify unintended effects |
| Causal Layered Analysis (CLA) | `Week 8`, p. 18 | Events, roles, priorities/power and deeper myths/metaphors | Layered reframing | Interpretive depth is not participant evidence | Keep layers and evidence types distinct |

## Supporting methods

| Method | Course anchor | Use | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| How Might We | `Week 5`, pp. 8–16; formula p. 16 | Convert synthesised insights into open, positive, outcome-focused opportunity questions | Do not embed a solution in the question |
| Futures Cone | `Week 3`, p. 11 | Distinguish possible, plausible, probable and preferable futures | A preferable future is not a forecast |
| Futures Triangle | `Week 3`, p. 12 | Map pulls of the future, pushes of the present and weights of the past | State which forces are evidenced or assumed |
| Human / non-human stakeholder mapping | `Week 3`, pp. 23–24 | Expand actors beyond direct human users and examine dynamics | Do not treat representation as proof of agency |
| Mash-up ideation | `Week 5`, pp. 18–27 | Generate lateral concepts by combining unrelated categories | Not a substitute for evidence-led selection |
| Provotype | `Week 8`, pp. 21, 39 | Materialise a tension to provoke reflection and make thinking visible | Pair provocation with a learning question and debrief |
| Winterhouse Social Design Pathways Matrix | `Week 10`, p. 22 | Compare scales with incremental or transformative change | Locate the intervention before claiming impact |

## Selection principle

```text
Systems Mapping → Three Horizons → Theory of Change
             → Design Experiments → Futures / Stakeholder checks
             → Change Map when sequencing is justified
```

Do not present this chain as evidence. Present it as a methodological process applied to traceable project evidence.
